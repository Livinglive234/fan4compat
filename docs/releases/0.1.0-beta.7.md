# Fan4Compat 0.1.0-beta.7

Candidate fix for Freecam stopping at the full-chunk boundary while DH displays
terrain beyond it. The enabled, active camera may continue its inherited player
tick when its terrain chunk is absent. Real players and inactive cameras keep the
native check. No additional chunk loading or LOD block interaction is introduced.

Requires Freecam's internal version 1.3.0+mc1.21 and DH 3.3.3. The 1.21.1 Freecam
download identifies itself with that internal version. No VS or IP is required;
the hook is client-only and skips absent or unsupported targets.

Validation: clean build, camera identity/enabled-state and real-player boundaries,
optional-mod gates, packaging and bytecode checks. Native Minecraft 1.21.1 and
Freecam selectors/APIs verified. In-game testing remains pending: cross the render
distance boundary, return, disable Freecam, and verify normal player movement.
The specific DH behavior exposing the vanilla tick condition is not established.
