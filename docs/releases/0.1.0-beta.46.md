# Fan4Compat 0.1.0-beta.46

**Aether capes no longer turn your skin layers back on.** Wearing a cape in the Aether's cape slot made your skin's jacket, sleeves, pants and hat layers show again even when you had switched them off in Skin Customization.
The Aether answers "is this skin layer shown?" with yes for every layer whenever a cape is equipped, instead of only for the cape. A small hook now runs ahead of the Aether's and, for every layer except the cape, gives the
answer vanilla gives from your own settings; the cape itself is left to the Aether. Switch: **Aether capes keep your skin layers** (`aetherCapeSkinLayers`, on by default, restart to change; needs the Aether).

Validation: clean build, all tests (new: the generated hook run against stand-ins for every layer and several customization values, the cape left alone, and its priority below the Aether's), and a check that every Minecraft member it
uses exists in the real 1.21.1 jar. Not run in game: that the hook runs before the Aether's relies on Mixin applying the lower priority number first.
