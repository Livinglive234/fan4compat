# Fan4Compat 0.1.0-beta.51

**Spatial GUI next to Immersive Portals: portals no longer go blank while a Spatial GUI screen is open.** Spatial GUI moves the camera to its own view of the player whenever a screen is open, at the end of `Camera.setup`. Immersive
Portals sets up the camera for each portal view at the same point and places it from the portal, so Spatial GUI was overwriting the portal camera's position with the player's and the portal showed nothing. A new late hook on
`Camera.setup` (it runs after both mods) has Immersive Portals place the camera again, so a portal view keeps its position from the portal and Spatial GUI's angles. It is part of the existing **Spatial GUI keeps its screen steady
beside portals** switch (`spatialGuiPortalFix`, on by default, restart to change), which also keeps the beta 50 fix for the screen being drawn with the portal's camera. Spatial GUI 1.9.1 only. One failure turns the camera hook off for the
session with a warning in the log.

**Known:** when you sweep the mouse quickly with a Spatial GUI screen open, a thin see-through edge can show at the sides of a portal that has blocks against both of its sides (noticed on the TARDIS room portals). Not yet
understood; the portal and main cameras agree in the angles and positions the trace captured at rest.

**README restructure.** Supported builds now sit directly under the summary and the links, followed by a short feature list; the long per-release feature narratives were removed (the details remain in `docs/` and in the git history). The
supported-builds table also lists Xaero's Minimap and World Map, Spatial GUI, Point Blank, Player Graves, and the mods with no version check.

Validation: clean build, all tests (including the camera hook's failure handling and the feature gates) and a real-jar check of every Spatial GUI, Minecraft and Immersive Portals member the hooks and the trace use. Run in game on a
Windows PC with Spatial GUI 1.9.1 and Immersive Portals 6.0.7: portals visible with the screen open and the main view unchanged.
