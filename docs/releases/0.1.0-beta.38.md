# Fan4Compat 0.1.0-beta.38

Xaero's **minimap and waypoints now follow the TARDIS exterior** (client only; Xaero's Minimap 26.5.0, World Map 1.46.0 and
Doctor Who Mod). While you are inside a TARDIS that has landed, Xaero treats you as standing at the exterior:

- Waypoints belong to the outside dimension: the waypoint list, new waypoints, the waypoint dialog's default coordinates and the
  temporary-waypoint hotkey. This uses the same world switch the waypoint menu's world selector makes.
- The minimap centers on the exterior and draws waypoints relative to it. Its terrain is what the World Map has stored there; the
  outside is not loaded on your client, so unmapped areas stay blank.
- The World Map keeps viewing the exterior's dimension while you are inside, loads regions around it, and marks you there.

In flight, outside a TARDIS, or when the exterior is unknown, everything is normal and anything this changed is put back (a world or
dimension you chose yourself is never touched). On by default, with a switch "Xaero minimap and waypoints follow the TARDIS
exterior" in the Fan4Compat settings. Not covered: the radar, exteriors in differently scaled dimensions (the Nether), live terrain.
Includes beta 36 (Xaero waypoint sync, unpublished) and beta 37 (World Map on the exterior).

Validation: clean build and regressions pass, with fixture tests for the location helper (player-only position, dimension and
waypoint world switched and undone, own choices kept, failures contained) and the map screen. `xaeroMapNativeCheck` binds all seven
Xaero hooks to the real Minimap and World Map classes and checks every Xaero, Doctor Who Mod (1.0.38.4 and 1.0.39) and Minecraft
member used. Not run in game. This is a test artifact; auto-updaters ignore it until published.
